Core Projects

Sep 2022 - Dec 2022

e-ROUTES

Purpose: Enhancing EV navigation for seamless charging experiences. As UX Designer for the Co-Pilote EV app, I crafted intuitive navigation through benchmarking, innovative visualisations, and iterative refinement.

Now live, aiding EV drivers in finding and navigating to stations efficiently.

Dec 2022 - Jul 2023

Space App

As UX Designer and Researcher, I led usability testing for next-gen EV vehicle mobile apps' core features, such as for the Lancia Ypsilon. Over seven months, I conducted 40+ usability tests and generated four detailed feedback reports to refine ADAS, EV scheduling, and remote control features.

My work contributed to a more user-centered experience through qualitative, quantitative, and data-driven insights.

Sep 2021 - Sep 2022

Conception Skin

Purpose: Synchronise multiple brands in one cohesive UI Kit, setting the foundation for the next generation of mobile apps with a unified, future-ready design language.

As UI Designer for this multi-brand design system, I built a clear, documented, and flexible component library from scratch. The result is a versatile "conception skin" that maintains consistency while supporting brand-specific needs and usability for all teams.

Article

Mar 2023 - Jul 2023

My Ami Play

As a UX Designer and Researcher, I led the design and interface integration of the Citroën Ami Mobile App. I analyzed user requirements, designed user flows, created interactive prototypes, and spearheaded research on haptic feedback.

The My Ami model lacks built-in interfaces and relies entirely on the mobile app, which is now successfully deployed in Citroën Ami vehicles.
